# SolarStructureModeling

- Original (KO): https://support.midasuser.com/hc/ko/articles/60714775742489-SolarStructureModeling
- Video: [20_SolarStructureModeling.mp4](../videos/20_SolarStructureModeling.mp4)
- GEN NX API CHALLENGE 2026

---

A plug-in that lets you pick a solar structure type (CASE 1–4) in MIDAS GEN NX, and
once you enter the dimensions, materials, and sections, automatically generates the
nodes, beam elements, and boundary conditions in the GEN NX model.

## Features

**01. Model input**
A plug-in that, once you enter the dimensions, materials, and sections, automatically
generates the nodes, beam elements, and boundary conditions in the GEN NX model.

**02. Model computation**
Once the installation type and key dimensions are entered, the nodes and elements
for every component — columns, roof beams, braces, purlins, and so on — are
automatically computed and generated, sharply cutting model-build time.

**03. Member computation**
Compared to manual modeling, the repetitive work of computing node numbers,
determining member coordinates, and entering boundary conditions disappears,
significantly reducing model-generation time.

**04. Load computation**
Gravity loads (dead, live, snow) and KDS-based wind loads are automatically computed
and assigned, reducing load-input errors and ensuring compliance with the design
code.
